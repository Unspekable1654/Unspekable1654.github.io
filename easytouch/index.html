Features

What EasyTouch does

Handwriting

Write a word with your finger and it is typed where you are. Digits only for codes, or sums: write 12 x 7 = and get 84.

Gestures

Hold Ctrl+Alt and draw: S signs a document, C takes a screenshot, D opens Downloads. Add your own.

Number pad

The touchpad becomes a 4 by 4 number pad for Excel, forms and banking sites, with a running total.

Screen pen

Draw on your screen with a laser that fades, a marker, or shapes that straighten themselves.

Palm safe

A resting finger or a palm types nothing. Esc always turns a mode off.

Works with ASUS NumberPad

Laptops with a built-in NumberPad keep it. EasyTouch never changes it.

How it works

Three steps

1

Press a shortcut

For example Ctrl+Alt+N for the number pad. A small pill shows which mode is on.

2

Use your finger

Write, tap or draw on the touchpad. The pointer stays still.

3

Press it again

The same shortcut, Esc, or 30 seconds without a touch turns it off.

Handy keys

Ctrl+Alt+NNumber pad

Private by design

Like every Easy Suite app: no account, no internet needed, no tracking.

  • Handwriting is read by Windows on this PC. Keys typed are never logged or stored.
  • The pointer is only held still while a mode is on, and always released.
  • No network access and no telemetry.
