EasySign

Available · version 1.5.2

Sign with your finger, right on your laptop.

  • Your touchpad becomes a signature pad. No printer, no scanner, no phone.
  • Sign PDFs and photos of forms, add the date, and save a signed copy.
  • No touchpad? Type your name in a handwriting look, or use a mouse or pen.

  • Make PDFs and photos small enough for email.

EasyClip

Everything you copied, and every screenshot, one shortcut away.
