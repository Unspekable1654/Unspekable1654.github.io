Features

What EasyNote does

One button to record

Press Record meeting, or Ctrl+Alt+M anywhere. Live meters, a mute for each side, pause and a timer.

The transcript writes itself

When you stop, every line is written down with its time, on your PC.

Who said what

Your microphone and the people in the call are kept apart, so each line is marked You or Others.

Markers

Press M to mark a moment you want to find again.

Find it again

Search the transcript, click a line to hear it, and fix a word with a double-click.

Yours to keep

Save it as a text file, subtitles or a Word document. Everything stays in your EasyNote folder.

How it works

Three steps

1

Record

Before a call, press Record meeting and tell the others you are recording.

2

Stop

EasyNote writes down what was said. A long meeting takes a few minutes.

3

Read and share

Search it, rename the speakers, and save it as text or a Word document.

Handy keys

Ctrl+Alt+MStart or stop recording
MMark this moment

Private by design

Like every Easy Suite app: no account, no internet needed, no tracking.

  • Records only while you record. It never starts by itself.
  • The transcript is made on your PC by a speech model that comes with the app.
  • No network access and no telemetry.
  • Found a problem or have an idea? Feedback is one click away inside the app: it opens a short form in your browser, and the app itself sends nothing.
