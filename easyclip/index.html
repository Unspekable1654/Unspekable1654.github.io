Features

What EasyClip does

EasyClip now includes EasyShot, so screenshots and everything you copy live in one place.

Clipboard history

Everything you copy, text and pictures, in a picker at the mouse. Type to search, press Enter to paste.

Secrets don't stay

One-time codes, card numbers, IBANs and passwords are never saved, and cleared after 30 seconds.

Paste queue

Copy several things from an email, then each Ctrl+V pastes the next one. Made for filling in forms.

Snippets

Saved text with fields like {date} filled in when you paste, for replies you type every day.

Screenshots

Drag an area, click a window or take the whole screen. Every screenshot lands in your history too.

Mark it up

Arrows, boxes, highlights, text, numbered steps, blur, redaction and crop.

Safe to share

It reads the screenshot on your PC and offers to cover emails, phone and card numbers in one click.

Pin and fix

Pin a screenshot on top of everything, or paste text as plain text, one line or trimmed.

How it works

Three steps

1

Copy as usual

Or press Ctrl+Alt+P to take a screenshot.

2

Press Ctrl+Alt+V

The picker opens at the mouse with everything you copied.

3

Pick and paste

Enter pastes into the window you were in. E opens a screenshot in the editor.

Private by design

Like every Easy Suite app: no account, no internet needed, no tracking.

  • History is encrypted for your Windows account and never leaves the PC.
  • Items that password managers mark as private are never recorded.
  • Text in screenshots is read by Windows on this PC, never by a cloud service.

Installing

EasyClip is new and made by one person, so Windows may be cautious about it at first. This is normal.

  1. If your browser says the file is not commonly downloaded, choose Keep.
  2. Right-click the downloaded file, choose Properties, tick Unblock and click OK. If there is no Unblock box, skip this.
  3. Double-click it. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Accept the licence agreement and follow the steps.
