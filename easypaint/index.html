Features

What EasyPaint does

Your touchpad is a pen

Press Space, then draw with one finger. Touch to draw, lift to stop, the same way EasySign signs.

Brushes that feel good

Smooth lines in every colour, with no jagged corners. Pencil, ink, marker, watercolour and more are on the way.

Open and save pictures

Draw on a new picture or open one you have. Save it as PNG, JPEG or a bitmap.

Private by design

Like every Easy Suite app: no account, no internet needed, no tracking.

  • Draws only on its own canvas, never over your other apps.
  • Your pictures stay on your PC.
  • No network access and no telemetry.
  • Found a problem or have an idea? Feedback opens a short form in your browser, and the app itself sends nothing.
